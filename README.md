### Hi, I'm zin294

I build small free web tools that each do one thing and show their sources.

**PullSim** — [pullsim.com](https://pullsim.com/)<br>
A free Pokémon pack opening simulator. Every pull rate comes from logged packs (2,000 per set) and is published with its sample size and confidence interval. [How the rates are calculated](https://pullsim.com/how-we-calculate/)

**Concrete Price Calculator** — [concretepricecalculator.com](https://concretepricecalculator.com/)<br>
Slab dimensions in, cubic yards and 80 lb bags out — plus an installed cost range benchmarked to a dated source and adjusted for metro labor wages. [Concrete cost per yard](https://concretepricecalculator.com/calculators/concrete-cost-per-yard/)

**RecastMate** — [recastmate.com](https://recastmate.com/)<br>
A mortgage recast calculator. Enter your balance, rate, term and lump sum to see the new payment and the interest saved. Everything runs in your browser.
